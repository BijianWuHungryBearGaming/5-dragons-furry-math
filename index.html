Every dragon combination

Each set is forced on every spin and resolved in order (Earth, Wood, Tide, Fire, Metal). Base game values exclude any bonus. Free spin values are a full 8-spin round with that loadout.

Bonus buy

Average value of a purchased round (no trigger spin) and the price that would return 96%.

Ante bet

A higher stake in exchange for more scatter chances.

Rules the simulator enforces

  • Scatters: at most one per reel. Exactly 3 scatters anywhere turn wild (near miss) before dragons resolve and the spin pays.
  • Trigger spin: a spin with 4 or more scatters has no dragons.
  • Free spins: always 8 spins, no retrigger, and no scatters land during the round.
  • Dragon order: Earth, Wood, Tide, Fire, pay, then Metal multiplies the total.
  • Cap: a paid spin or bonus round stops at 8,888x.
